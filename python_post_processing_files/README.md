## Notebooks
The following Jupyter notebooks are provided for reproducing figures and analysis:

- **`fft.ipynb`** – Frequency analysis (FFT) of probe signals to extract vortex shedding frequency and Strouhal number.
- **`fig6a_Axial_vel_spanwise average.ipynb`** – Spanwise-averaged axial velocity (Fig. 6a of MVP paper).
- **`fig6b_axial_vel_rms.ipynb`** – RMS of axial velocity fluctuations (Fig. 6b).
- **`fig8_rs.ipynb`** – Reynolds stress components (Fig. 8).
- **`fig9a_axial_vel.ipynb`** – Streamwise velocity profiles (Fig. 9a).
- **`fig9b_anistropy.ipynb`** – Turbulence anisotropy analysis (Fig. 9b).
- **`fig9c_fluctuation.ipynb`** – Turbulence fluctuations (Fig. 9c).
